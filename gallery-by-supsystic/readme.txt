== Description ==

**Photo Gallery by Supsystic** is a WordPress gallery plugin for responsive photo galleries, image galleries, image carousels and sliders. Tell the creation wizard what the gallery is for, add images from the Media Library in bulk, drag them into order, pick a ready style and publish it with a Gutenberg block, an Elementor widget or a shortcode. No code required.

Unlimited galleries, carousels, sliders and images are free.

**Try it in your browser:** [open a live demo in WordPress Playground](https://playground.wordpress.net/?plugin=gallery-by-supsystic) – no install, no account.

= Three gallery types =

* **Gallery** – a responsive photo gallery in a grid, masonry, justified rows or horizontal layout, with a lightbox and albums.
* **Carousel** – an image carousel with several photos in a row, arrows, dots, autoplay and swipe. Also a logo carousel and a WooCommerce product carousel.
* **Slider** – a full-width image slider, hero slider or slideshow with text, buttons and a thumbnail strip.

Each type has its own editor with a live preview for desktop, tablet and phone.

= Creation wizard =

Choose what you are building – a masonry gallery, an image carousel, a logo marquee, a hero slider, an image viewer with thumbnails or a product carousel – add your images and get a finished gallery in about a minute. Change anything later in the editor.

= Ready-made styles and templates =

Every type comes with ready styles: colors, corners, shadows, arrows, dots and captions in one click. Switch the style any time, your images stay where they are.

Show your WooCommerce products in a product carousel or product slider with price, rating, sale badge and an Add to cart button. Show the newest, most popular or top-rated products, or pick them by hand. Cards follow your style or the look of your shop.

= Lightbox =

A modern, responsive lightbox for every gallery type: swipe, zoom, fullscreen mode and a counter, in the colors of your style. Three lightbox themes are included.

= Albums with Gallery Groups =

Gather several galleries into one album that visitors can browse. Gallery Groups work well for portfolios, events, client folders, projects and travel albums.

== Installation ==

1. In your dashboard go to Plugins → Add New Plugin and search for "Photo Gallery by Supsystic".
2. Click Install Now, then Activate.
3. Open the Photo Gallery menu and click Add new.
4. Choose what you are building in the wizard: a gallery, a carousel or a slider.
5. Add images from the Media Library, pick a style, turn on the extras you need and save.
6. Add it to a page with the block, the Elementor widget or the gallery shortcode.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is the free version limited in galleries or images? =

No. You can create unlimited galleries, carousels and sliders with unlimited images. The free plugin never expires.

= Can I make an image carousel or slider? =

Yes. Carousel and Slider are gallery types of their own: image carousels, logo carousels, hero sliders and thumbnail sliders, with autoplay, swipe, arrows, dots and text layers. Pro adds 3D effects, video slides and before and after sliders.

= Which gallery layouts are free? =

Standard grid, masonry, justified rows, rounded thumbnails, horizontal layout, horizontal scroll galleries and fixed columns, with separate column counts for desktop, tablet and mobile. Mosaic is in Pro.

= Can I show WooCommerce products? =

Yes. Product carousels and sliders show price, rating, sale badge and an Add to cart button. Leaving out products or whole categories is in Pro.

= Can I create albums? =

Yes. Gallery Groups combine several galleries into one album that visitors can browse.

= Does it include a lightbox? =

Yes. Every gallery type opens images in a modern lightbox with swipe, zoom and fullscreen mode. Pro adds a slideshow, more lightbox themes, thumbnails, download and share.

= Will my existing galleries change after the update? =

No. Your galleries and their settings stay exactly as they were. The new types, styles and wizard are there when you create something new.

= Can I add many images at once? =

Yes. Select any number of images in the Media Library and add them in one step, then drag them into order.

= Are the galleries responsive? =

Yes. Galleries, carousels and sliders adapt to every screen, and you can set different columns, slides in a row and heights for desktop, tablet and mobile.

= How do I add a gallery to a page or post? =

Use the plugin's block in the block editor, the Elementor widget, or paste the gallery shortcode into any page, post or widget area.

= Does it work with my theme and page builder? =

The plugin works with modern WordPress themes and has a native Gutenberg block and Elementor widget. In Divi, Beaver Builder and other page builders, use the shortcode.

= Can I add videos to a gallery? =

Video galleries and video slides with YouTube, Vimeo and video files are available in Pro.

= Can I add a watermark or sell photos? =

Yes, with Pro: watermark, right-click protection, paid access to galleries or selected images, paid resolution downloads and private galleries.

= Will it slow down my site? =

Carousel and slider scripts load only where they are shown. Use image sizes that match your layout and the gallery stays light. For very large galleries, Pro adds lazy load, pagination, Load More, image optimization and CDN delivery.

= How can I report a security issue? =

Please report security issues through the [Patchstack Vulnerability Disclosure Program](https://patchstack.com/database/vdp/9e5fb455-a563-42ff-bd88-ba7e56ed6014). The Patchstack team validates and triages each report and notifies us.
